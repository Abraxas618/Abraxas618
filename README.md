## About

Baramay Station Research Inc. is an independent Canadian non-profit laboratory conducting exploratory research in:

* post-quantum cryptography
* zero-knowledge and verifiable computation
* distributed and mesh systems
* deterministic execution and zkVMs
* digital twins and simulation
* experimental ledger and state architectures

Research published here is **experimental, non-production, and subject to revision**. Cryptographic constructions should not be considered secure or validated without independent review.

---

## TetraKlein

**TetraKlein: A Unified Architecture** is the laboratory's primary research program.

It investigates a unified computational architecture combining:

* Post-Quantum Cryptography
* ZK-STARKs and IVC
* Verifiable Computation
* Deterministic zkVMs
* Mesh Networking
* Digital Twins and XR Simulation
* Hypercube Ledger Models

**Research question:**

> Can computation, identity, state transitions, and simulation outputs be independently verifiable under post-quantum security assumptions?
